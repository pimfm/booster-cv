# BN-02 — Progress header

**Phase** 2 · **Lane** D · **Size** S · **Depends on** BN-01

## Goal
Progress legible at a glance without a progress-bar cliché.

## Owns
`src/binder/Progress.tsx`, `Progress.module.css`.

## Scope
- `collected / total` in display face; a segmented bar with one segment per pack, each segment filled proportionally to that pack's collected count (flat fill in the pack tint, `--border-1` between segments). Segment fill animates with `spring.settle`.
- When complete: the count gets a filled square and strings.binder_complete; no animation beyond the segment fill.
- Mobile: compact form (`7/24` + mini bar) in the strip's left end.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] RTL: counts and per-pack fill derived from state; complete state text present at `all-collected`.
- [ ] Screenshot `BN-02-progress-*.png`.
