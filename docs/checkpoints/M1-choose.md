# M1 — choose a direction

Two style tiles, real CSS, same sections, same content. Pick one and D-02 freezes it into tokens.

**How to view:** `npm run dev`, then open `/dev/direction/1` and `/dev/direction/2`
(a switch at the top of each page jumps between them). Screenshots are in `e2e/__screens__/D-01-*.png`.

## 1 — Print shop

- Warm cream paper, near-black ink, mustard (action) and cobalt (focus, rare).
- Archivo Black for headings (uppercase) and card titles (sentence case); Inter for text.
- Stage: a faint 20 px dot grid, like a cutting mat.
- Rarity: the colour of the frame around the card (beige, sage, cobalt, mustard).
- Placeholder art: tint fill, crop mark, outlined initial.
- Feel: calm, editorial, familiar. Safest of the two.
- Details: `docs/design/direction-1.md`.

## 2 — Score pad

- Cool off-white paper, deep navy ink, tomato (action, focus, rare) and mint (box, on-states).
- Bricolage Grotesque 800 in sentence case, set larger; IBM Plex Sans for text.
- Stage: plain paper, with one oversized 16 px shadow under the tray.
- Rarity: same ink border on every card, plus a coloured corner stamp.
- Cards have an ink title bar; placeholder art is a filled initial with a hard shadow.
- Feel: brisker, more contemporary product UI. More character, a little more risk.
- Details: `docs/design/direction-2.md`.
